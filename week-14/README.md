# Week 14 – Video Game Sales Dashboard

![Bar Plot](/week-14/dashboard.png)

## About the Project

This project provides a comprehensive exploration of the global video game industry, uncovering the underlying trends and narratives driven by decades of sales data. Rather than just looking at numbers, it delves into the "story" of how gaming has evolved as a dominant cultural and economic force.

---

## Goal

The primary objective of this study is to perform an in-depth analysis of video game sales through advanced data visualization techniques. By utilizing various analytical charts, the project aims to identify market patterns, compare performance across different platforms and genres, and derive actionable insights into consumer behavior over time.

---

## Dataset

* **Source:** [Video Game Sales](https://www.kaggle.com/datasets/gregorut/videogamesales)
* **Description:**   This dataset contains a list of video games with sales greater than 100,000 copies. It was generated by a scrape of vgchartz.com. (Based on the original dataset description)

> The raw dataset is not included in this repository. Please refer to the original Kaggle source.

## What Was Done

- I analyzed the total product count categorized by publisher and genre to identify the most active players and popular categories in the industry.

- I identified the top 10 most successful video games by evaluating their total global sales performance.

- I conducted a historical review to determine the top 10 years with the highest global sales volume.

- I utilized a pie chart to visualize the distribution of market share among different gaming devices to identify the most preferred platforms.
